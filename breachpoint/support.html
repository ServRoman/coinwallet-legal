FAQ

How do I give orders? Pause, select a fighter, draw his route and add actions at the waypoints (open, kick, flashbang, wait for a code). Unpause to watch the plan work.

What are tokens? Tokens save time instead of watching a video: double the mission pay, put a fighter back on duty, speed up building or research. They never make you stronger in a fight.

I bought tokens but didn’t get them. Open Headquarters → Tokens and tap “Restore purchases”.

How do I get “No ads” on a new device? Headquarters → Tokens → “Restore purchases” with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Do I need an account? No. Your career stays on your device.
