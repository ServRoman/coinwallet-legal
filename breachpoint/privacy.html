Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Breach Point: SWAT Tactics (the “App”) is a tactical game in which you plan and lead a police special unit. It is published by CRYPTOKYIV (the “Developer”). Contact: itsovetnik.biz@gmail.com.

Your game stays on your device

Your career (squad, money, tokens, headquarters, unlocked gear) and your settings are stored only on your phone or tablet. We do not create accounts and do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. The App does not send gameplay analytics.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch, and an occasional video between missions. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier and IP address to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example, in the EEA and the UK), the App asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice. On iPhone and iPad the App does not ask to track you, so ads are not personalized with the advertising identifier. Buying “No ads” removes the ads between missions. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Tokens and “No ads” are bought through Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your payment details. To deliver and restore purchases the App uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase receipts. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the App erases your career from the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: itsovetnik.biz@gmail.com.
