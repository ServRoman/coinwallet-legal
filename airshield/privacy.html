Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Air Shield: Missile Defense (the “App”) is a strategy game in which you defend a city from air attacks. It is published by CRYPTOKYIV (the “Developer”). Contact: itsovetnik.biz@gmail.com.

Your game stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, credits, gold, upgrades, skins) and your settings are stored only on your phone. We do not create accounts and do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob: a video between some battles and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier and IP address to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example, in the EEA and the UK), the App asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice. On iPhone the App does not ask to track you, so ads are not personalized with the advertising identifier. Buying “No ads” removes the ads between battles. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics and crash reports

To improve the game we use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. They receive anonymous usage events (for example, which level was played and whether it was won), basic device information (model, OS version, language) and crash reports. This data is not linked to your identity and is not sold.

Purchases

Gold and “No ads” are bought through Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your payment details. To deliver and restore purchases the App uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase receipts. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the App erases your progress from the phone. To delete analytics data linked to your device, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: itsovetnik.biz@gmail.com.

Реклама

Приложение показывает рекламу через Google AdMob: видео между некоторыми боями и видео за награду, которые вы включаете сами. AdMob может использовать рекламный идентификатор устройства и IP-адрес, чтобы показывать и измерять рекламу. См. policies.google.com/privacy.

Чтобы улучшать игру, мы используем Google Firebase Analytics и Crashlytics. Они получают анонимные события игры (например, какой уровень сыгран и выигран ли он), основные данные об устройстве (модель, версия ОС, язык) и отчёты о сбоях. Эти данные не связаны с вашей личностью и не продаются.
