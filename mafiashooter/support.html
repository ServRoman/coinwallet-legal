FAQ

How do I play? Left side of the screen — move. Hold FIRE to shoot; PUNCH and KICK work up close; DODGE rolls you out of the line of fire. Doors open when you walk into them; a kick knocks them down.

I bought gold but didn’t get it. Open the Shop and tap “Restore purchases”.

How do I get “No ads” on a new device? Shop → “Restore purchases” with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Does it work offline? Yes. Only ads and purchases need the internet.

Do I need an account? No. Your progress stays on your device.
